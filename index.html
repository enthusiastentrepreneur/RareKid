The fit

One size. Cut to fit the few.

No size charts, no guessing, no "should I go up?". Every Rare Kid tee is built on a medium and opened up into a true oversized shape: dropped shoulders, a wide boxy body and sleeves that fall past the shoulder.

That's the point of one size. On a smaller frame it hangs loose and slouchy, the way an oversized tee should. On a broader frame it sits closer, like a clean relaxed tee. Same piece, your shape.

Best for anyone who'd normally wear S to L. If you usually take XL or bigger, expect a fitted rather than oversized look. Not sure? Ask us on WhatsApp.
