7. Returns: your 14-day right to cancel

Under the EU Consumer Rights Directive and the Irish Consumer Rights Act 2022, you can cancel your order for any reason within 14 days of the day you receive it.

How to return

  1. Tell us within 14 days of delivery by WhatsApp (089 972 1947) or email (thefew@rarekid.store), with your order number. A simple message saying you want to cancel is enough, or you can use the form at the end of this page.
  2. Send the tee back to us within 14 days of telling us, to the address we give you. We recommend a tracked service and keeping your proof of postage.

What you pay, and what we refund

  • You pay the cost of returning the tee to us. Sending it with An Post within Ireland normally costs only a few euro.
  • We refund the full price of the tee and the standard delivery cost you paid, within 14 days of receiving the tee back, or of receiving your proof of postage, whichever comes first.
  • We refund using the same payment method you used: back to your bank account for bank transfers, or through PayPal for PayPal payments, unless you agree otherwise. There is no fee for the refund.

Condition of returns

You can try the tee on the way you would in a shop. If it's been worn beyond that, washed, damaged, or comes back with odours or marks, we may reduce your refund to reflect the lost value. We'll explain any deduction to you.

Please return the numbered card and stickers with the tee. If they aren't returned, we may deduct a fair amount for them.
