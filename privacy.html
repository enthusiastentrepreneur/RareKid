Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

Rare Kid respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, how long we keep it, who we share it with, and the rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Irish Data Protection Act 2018 and the ePrivacy Regulations (S.I. No. 336 of 2011).

Short version: we collect only what we need to tell you about drops and to deliver your order. We never sell your data. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies unless you say yes.

3. Who we share it with

We only share your data with services that help us run Rare Kid, and only what they need:

  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited, part of Meta): when you place an order, your order details go to us as a WhatsApp message from your own account. WhatsApp's privacy policy applies to its service.
  • Formspree (Formspree, Inc., USA): receives sign-up form entries, stores them securely and forwards them to our inbox.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts this website and keeps basic server logs.
  • Our email provider: stores the emails we send and receive.
  • An Post: receives your name, address and contact details to deliver your order.
  • PayPal (PayPal (Europe) S.à r.l. et Cie, S.C.A.): processes payments made through our PayPal payment links. We never see your card details.
  • Our bank: receives bank transfers and the payer name and reference that come with them.
  • Meta (Facebook): only if you choose to message us there. Meta's own privacy policy applies.
  • Revenue or other authorities: only where the law requires it.

We never sell or rent your personal data.

4. Transfers outside the EU

Some of these services may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on the safeguards the GDPR allows, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

5. How long we keep it

  • Sign-up list: until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. If we haven't emailed you about a drop in 24 months, we delete your details.
  • Orders: for 6 years after the end of the tax year, as Irish tax law requires.
  • Messages and WhatsApp chats: up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless they relate to an order. Order chats are kept with our order records.
  • Server logs: kept by our host for a short period for security.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can, free of charge:

  • Get a copy of the personal data we hold about you (access).
  • Have it corrected if it's wrong (rectification).
  • Have it deleted (erasure, "right to be forgotten").
  • Limit how we use it (restriction).
  • Get it in a portable format (portability).
  • Object to us using it, including for direct marketing at any time (objection).
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, for example by clicking "unsubscribe" in any email or replying "stop". This doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew.

Email us at thefew@rarekid.store and we'll reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

8. Children

Our sign-up list isn't intended for anyone under 16. If you're under 16, please don't send us your details. If we learn we hold a child's data without a parent's consent, we'll delete it.

9. Keeping your data safe

We use secure, password-protected accounts with two-step sign-in, HTTPS on this website, and we limit access to your data to the people who need it to fulfil your order.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we'll let our subscribers know by email.
